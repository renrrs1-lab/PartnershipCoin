# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

## Introduction

PartnershipCoin (PARC) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting users interacting with our application and ecosystem.

## Information We Process

The application may process:

- Public wallet addresses
- Public blockchain transaction history
- QR code payment information
- Basic application usage data

## Information We Do NOT Collect

PartnershipCoin does NOT collect or store:

- Private keys
- Seed phrases
- Passwords
- Government identification
- Banking information

## Wallet Connections

The app supports integrations with:

- MetaMask
- WalletConnect

Authentication and transaction signing are securely handled by the user's wallet provider.

## Blockchain Transparency

Transactions executed on public blockchain networks such as BNB Chain are public and permanently recorded on-chain.

## Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect application integrity and user interactions.

## Third-Party Services

The application may interact with:

- BNB Chain
- WalletConnect
- MetaMask
- Google Play Services

## Children's Privacy

The application is not intended for users under 13 years old.
