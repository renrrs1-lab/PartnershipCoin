# PartnershipCoin Whitepaper

## Introduction

PartnershipCoin (PARC) was created with the idea of transforming collaboration, trust, and partnerships into measurable value within digital communities and professional environments.

## Problem

Collaboration, assistance, and community participation often generate value without any transparent or measurable reward mechanism.

## Solution

PARC introduces a decentralized token-based ecosystem designed to reward participation, collaboration, and community engagement through blockchain technology.

## Potential Use Cases

* Community rewards
* Tips and appreciation payments
* Task incentives
* Participation rewards
* Ecosystem growth campaigns
* Partnership incentives

## Token Information

* Network: BNB Smart Chain
* Symbol: PARC
* Fixed Supply: 1,000,000 PARC
* Smart Contract: Verified on BscScan

## Vision

The long-term vision of PARC is to build a transparent ecosystem where collaboration and partnerships can generate measurable digital value for communities and users.
