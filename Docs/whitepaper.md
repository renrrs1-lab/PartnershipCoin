# PartnershipCoin Whitepaper

## 1. Introduction

PartnershipCoin (PARC) is a community-driven cryptocurrency built on BNB Smart Chain with a focus on collaboration, transparency and long-term ecosystem growth.

The idea behind PARC comes from a simple real-world concept: collaboration has value. PartnershipCoin aims to represent this value through a decentralized digital asset that can be used within communities, projects and future Web3 applications.

---

## 2. Problem

In many communities and professional environments, people collaborate, help each other, share knowledge and complete tasks without a simple way to recognize or reward that contribution.

Traditional systems often lack a transparent, simple and decentralized mechanism to represent small contributions, support or partnership-based interactions.

---

## 3. Solution

PartnershipCoin introduces PARC as a digital token designed to support collaboration-based interactions.

PARC can be used as a community asset for:

- rewards
- tips
- participation incentives
- community campaigns
- partnership incentives
- future app-based interactions

The goal is not to create unrealistic financial promises, but to build a transparent and useful ecosystem over time.

## 5. Tokenomics

The initial token distribution is structured as follows:

| Category | Allocation |
|---|---:|
| Liquidity | 20% |
| Community & Airdrop | 30% |
| Reserve Treasury | 40% |
| Team | 10% |

### Allocation Purpose

**Liquidity**  
Used to support trading availability and market access on decentralized exchanges.

**Community & Airdrop**  
Reserved for early users, community campaigns, participation rewards and future ecosystem growth.

**Reserve Treasury**  
Reserved for long-term sustainability, future integrations, development, ecosystem expansion and strategic needs.

**Team**  
Reserved for project contributors and long-term project maintenance.

---

## 6. Transparency

PartnershipCoin was created with transparency as a core principle.

Key transparency points:

- Fixed total supply
- Public smart contract
- Verified contract on BscScan
- Public trading through PancakeSwap
- Public documentation on GitHub
- Public market tracking through DexScreener and GeckoTerminal

The deployed contract does not expose administrative owner functions such as minting, pausing, blacklisting or transfer control.

## 8. Utility Vision

The long-term vision of PARC is to become a simple and accessible Web3 utility token focused on collaboration and community interaction.

Planned utility directions include:

- mobile app integration
- QRCode-based transfers
- MetaMask mobile connection
- simple wallet-to-wallet transfers
- community rewards
- participation incentives
- future partnership-based features

The goal is to create an experience similar to simple digital payments, while keeping user custody with their own Web3 wallets.
